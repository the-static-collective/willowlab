GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things you might be wondering.

Every home is different. Here are some useful starting points.

How much does a cleaning cost? +

Every home and project is different. Share a few details about your space and the type of cleaning you need, and Willow can follow up with a personalized estimate.

Do you offer one-time or recurring visits? +

The existing Willow website lists residential, deep, one-time, and move-out cleaning. Tell us the type and frequency you have in mind when you request an estimate.

What if I'm not sure which cleaning to choose? +

No problem. Describe what you're hoping to accomplish, and Willow can help determine whether regular home care, deep cleaning, or a move-out service is the best fit.

Can I ask about a town outside Mankato? +

Yes. Willow serves Mankato and surrounding Southern Minnesota communities. Share your city or ZIP code to confirm availability for your location.
